    ACE-031

    Unapproved Soluble activin receptor IIB Anti-doping: S4.3

    Group

    Substances around myostatin and muscle growth

    Do not combine: same claimed goal, no data

    Group

    What it is

    A fusion protein studied in muscular dystrophy.

    How it works

    Binds TGF-beta family ligands, including myostatin.

    Use

    Studied in muscle diseases.

    What the research shows

    Clinical trials were stopped due to side effects (nosebleeds and vascular changes).

    What to watch for

    Clinical data point to vascular risk.

    Anti-doping

    Listed by WADA in class S4.3, prohibited at all times. WADA

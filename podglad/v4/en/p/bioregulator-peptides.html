Bioregulator peptides

Pinealon, Vesugen, Cartalax, Cardiogen, Crystagen, Bronchogen, Cortagen

Unapproved Short peptides (2–4 amino acids)

Group

“Anti-ageing” substances (unapproved)

Do not combine: same claimed goal, no data

Group

What it is

Short sequences developed mainly in Russia. The best known is Pinealon (Glu-Asp-Arg), described as a brain bioregulator, from the St Petersburg Institute of Bioregulation and Gerontology.

How it works

Authors' hypothesis: binding to DNA and regulating gene expression in specific tissues.

Use

Described as supporting individual organs.

What the research shows

No large independent clinical trials.

What to watch for

@ND

About

No shop. No doses from strangers. No promises.

A reference for 123 peptides and related substances: what each one is, what the research actually shows, whether any regulator approved it, and which groups act on the same receptor. Nothing here is for sale, and we never publish doses for substances that are not approved medicines.

We publish doses only for approved medicines, straight from the label, and only as information — never as a recommendation.

For unapproved substances there is no dose to give. Where human safety is unestablished, that is the finding, not a gap to fill.

Educational content, not medical advice. Decisions about treatment belong with a doctor who knows your case.

How the catalog is built

Status first

Every entry starts with whether a regulator approved it, and for what. Everything else follows from that.

Sources, not adjectives

Claims link to labels, reviews and trial registries. Where evidence is thin, the entry says so.

No doses from strangers

Doses appear only for approved medicines, copied from the label, as information rather than advice.

    Steroids

    Steroids: what they are and what they do to the body

    A reference to anabolic steroids and the drugs sold alongside them: what each one is, what it was made for, how it is regulated and what the evidence says about the risks. Education only, with no doses and no places to buy.

    Compare all steroids Steroids vs peptides

    This section is information, not advice. We give no doses, no routes to buy and no instructions for use. Most of these drugs are controlled or prescription-only, and the safe way to any hormone treatment is a doctor.

    Two families, one name

    “Steroid” means a molecule with a four-ring core. Anabolic steroids copy testosterone and build muscle; corticosteroids copy cortisol and calm inflammation. They are different drugs with different risks.

    Not the same as peptides

    Peptides are short chains of amino acids. Steroids are fat-derived hormones. Some people lump them together because they appear in the same markets and the same banned lists.

    Why the risks are real

    High, repeated use of anabolic steroids is linked to heart, liver, hormonal and mental-health problems. Medical doses given for a diagnosis are a different situation from doses taken for looks or sport.

    Injectable anabolic steroids

    Testosterone and its relatives given by injection. A few are real medicines (testosterone replacement), others are veterinary products or old drugs that were withdrawn.

    Several formsTestosterone Testosterone is the body’s main androgen, made mostly in the testes in men and in small amounts in the ovaries and adrenal glands in women. InjectableTestosterone cypionate Testosterone cypionate is testosterone joined to a fatty acid chain, called an ester, which makes it release slowly from the injection site. InjectableTestosterone enanthate Testosterone enanthate is another long-acting ester of testosterone and behaves very much like cypionate. Several formsTestosterone undecanoate Testosterone undecanoate is an ester with an unusually long action. InjectableSustanon Sustanon is a brand-name blend of four different testosterone esters, from short to long acting, in one oil injection. InjectableNandrolone decanoate Nandrolone decanoate is a long-acting anabolic steroid made from a testosterone-like molecule with one methyl group removed. InjectableTrenbolone Trenbolone is a very potent anabolic steroid made for cattle, where it is used as a growth implant. InjectableBoldenone Boldenone is an anabolic steroid related to testosterone, developed for veterinary use, mainly in horses. InjectableDrostanolone Drostanolone is a derivative of dihydrotestosterone, the potent androgen made from testosterone. InjectableMethenolone enanthate Methenolone enanthate is a long-acting injectable form of a mild anabolic steroid derived from dihydrotestosterone. InjectableTestosterone propionate Testosterone propionate is a short-acting ester of testosterone. InjectableNandrolone phenylpropionate Nandrolone phenylpropionate is a short-acting ester of the anabolic steroid nandrolone. InjectableTrestolone Trestolone, also called MENT, is a very potent synthetic androgen made from nandrolone.

    Oral anabolic steroids

    Tablets and capsules. Most are chemically altered so they survive the liver, and that alteration is also why many of them are hard on it.

    OralOxandrolone Oxandrolone is an oral anabolic steroid with relatively weak androgenic effects. OralMethandienone Methandienone is one of the first anabolic steroids made for people, developed in the 1950s. Several formsStanozolol Stanozolol is a synthetic steroid derived from dihydrotestosterone, sold in tablet and injectable forms. OralOxymetholone Oxymetholone is a strong oral anabolic steroid that is still a licensed medicine in the US for certain anaemias. OralMethyltestosterone Methyltestosterone is testosterone with one extra methyl group so that it can be swallowed. OralChlorodehydromethyltestosterone This is a synthetic oral steroid developed in East Germany in the 1960s and sold as Oral-Turinabol. OralMesterolone Mesterolone is a mild oral androgen derived from dihydrotestosterone. OralDanazol Danazol is a synthetic steroid with weak androgen activity. OralMethasterone Methasterone is a strong oral steroid derived from dihydrotestosterone. TopicalClostebol Clostebol is an anabolic steroid made by adding a chlorine atom to testosterone.

    Topical, prohormone and other hormonal drugs

    Gels, precursors the body converts into hormones, and one menopause drug with anabolic activity. Different routes, same hormone system.

    TopicalTestosterone gel Testosterone gel is rubbed onto the skin, usually on the shoulders, arms or abdomen, and absorbed into the blood over the day. OralDHEA DHEA is a hormone made by the adrenal glands that the body turns into testosterone and oestrogen. OralAndrostenedione Androstenedione is a hormone made in the adrenal glands and gonads that is one step away from testosterone and oestrogen. OralTibolone Tibolone is a synthetic steroid used for menopause symptoms. TopicalDihydrotestosterone Dihydrotestosterone, or DHT, is a hormone the body makes from testosterone.

    SARMs and related “research chemicals”

    Selective androgen receptor modulators were designed to act on muscle and bone with fewer hormonal side effects than steroids. None has been approved, and regulators keep warning about products sold under these names.

    OralOstarine Ostarine, also called enobosarm, is the best-known SARM. OralLigandrol Ligandrol, or LGD-4033, is an experimental SARM that was tested in early human trials and never approved. OralRAD140 RAD140, also called testolone, is an experimental SARM that has only been studied in animals and lab work. OralAndarine Andarine, also called S-4, is an early experimental SARM that was never developed into a medicine. OralYK-11 YK-11 is a lab compound built on a steroid backbone that acts on the androgen receptor.

    Other drugs sold alongside steroids

    Growth hormone, stimulants, fat burners and lab compounds that show up in the same circles. They are not steroids, and some of them are far more dangerous than the steroids themselves.

    OralCardarine Cardarine, or GW501516, is often grouped with SARMs but is a different kind of compound, a PPAR-delta agonist. OralMK-677 MK-677, or ibutamoren, is an oral drug that raises growth hormone and IGF-1 by acting on the ghrelin receptor. OralSR9009 SR9009 is a lab compound that acts on Rev-Erb, a protein that helps run the body clock and energy use. InjectableSomatropin Somatropin is lab-made human growth hormone, a protein of 191 amino acids. OralClenbuterol Clenbuterol is a stimulant that opens the airways. OralDNP DNP, or 2,4-dinitrophenol, is an industrial chemical used in dyes and explosives. OralTadalafil Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor taken by mouth. OralSildenafil Sildenafil is the first PDE5 inhibitor, launched as Viagra in 1998. OralDapoxetine Dapoxetine is a short-acting antidepressant-type drug (an SSRI) made for premature ejaculation. OralYohimbine Yohimbine is an alkaloid from the bark of an African tree. OralLiothyronine Liothyronine is the active form of thyroid hormone, known as T3. OralPhentermine Phentermine is a stimulant related to amphetamine that reduces appetite. OralDMAA DMAA, or 1,3-dimethylamylamine, is a strong stimulant that was sold in pre-workout and “fat burner” supplements. Several formsSalbutamol Salbutamol, called albuterol in the US, is the common reliever inhaler for asthma. OralMeldonium Meldonium is a drug made in Latvia, sold in Eastern Europe as Mildronate. OralModafinil Modafinil is a prescription drug that promotes wakefulness. OralPiracetam Piracetam is the first of a family of compounds called racetams, and the drug that gave the word “nootropic” to the world. OralBAM15 BAM15 is a lab compound that makes mitochondria, the cell’s energy factories, burn fuel without making as much usable energy.

    Hormone-modulating medicines

    Real prescription drugs for breast cancer, fertility and hormone disorders. They show up in the steroid world because they change oestrogen or the signals that drive testosterone, but they are not steroids.

    OralClomiphene Clomiphene is a selective oestrogen receptor modulator (SERM) that blocks oestrogen signals in the brain. OralTamoxifen Tamoxifen is a SERM that blocks oestrogen’s action on breast tissue. OralAnastrozole Anastrozole is an aromatase inhibitor. OralLetrozole Letrozole is a strong aromatase inhibitor used mainly for breast cancer in women after menopause. InjectablehCG hCG is a protein hormone made by the placenta in pregnancy. OralRaloxifene Raloxifene is a SERM that acts like oestrogen on bone and blocks it in breast and womb tissue. OralExemestane Exemestane is a breast cancer drug that is itself built on a steroid backbone. OralCabergoline Cabergoline is a dopamine agonist that lowers the hormone prolactin. OralEnclomiphene Enclomiphene is one of the two forms (isomers) that make up clomiphene. OralFinasteride Finasteride is a prescription tablet that blocks the enzyme turning testosterone into DHT. OralDutasteride Dutasteride is a prescription capsule that blocks two forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT further than finasteride.

    Corticosteroids

    The other family called “steroids”. They calm inflammation and the immune system and do not build muscle; they are a different class from anabolic steroids and are mixed up with them all the time.

    OralPrednisone Prednisone is a corticosteroid taken by mouth. Several formsDexamethasone Dexamethasone is a very potent, long-acting corticosteroid, given by mouth, injection or drops. Several formsHydrocortisone Hydrocortisone is the medicine version of cortisol, the hormone the adrenal glands make every day. Several formsPrednisolone Prednisolone is the active form of prednisone and is widely prescribed as tablets, liquid for children, eye drops and injections.

    The July 2026 FDA committee vote: what it really means for BPC-157 and others

    On 22–23 July 2026 the FDA Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee (PCAC) reviewed seven peptides. It backed six: BPC-157, TB-500 and KPV (each 8 to 6 with one abstention), plus MOTS-c, Semax and Epitalon. It rejected DSIP (emideltide) 6 to 7.

    What it means: the committee recommends adding these substances to the 503A list of bulk ingredients US pharmacies may compound on an individual prescription. The recommendation is non-binding. The FDA still has to run its own process, estimated at several months to over a year.

    What it does not mean: it is not drug approval. There are no phase 3 trials, established doses or indications. FDA staff raised safety and evidence concerns, and the votes were close. It applies only to US prescription compounding, not to “research” products from online shops or blends such as GLOW or KLOW.

    In Poland and the EU these substances are still not authorised as medicines. We will update the PepSafe catalog when the FDA makes a final decision.

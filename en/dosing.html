    Semaglutide

    Ozempic (semaglutide, injection)

    Indication: Type 2 diabetes; reduction of cardiovascular and kidney outcomes in type 2 diabetes

    Start
    Start: 0.25 mg once weekly for 4 weeks (initiation only, not therapeutic). Then 0.5 mg. If needed, after at least 4 weeks 1 mg, then after another 4 weeks 2 mg.
    Maintenance
    Maintenance: 0.5, 1 or 2 mg (1 mg in CKD). Max 2 mg/week. Subcutaneous, once weekly, same day.
    Notes
    Missed dose: give within 5 days; after 5 days skip it.

    Label revision: 06/2026 · Sources

    Semaglutide

    Wegovy (semaglutide, injection)

    Indication: Chronic weight management; cardiovascular risk reduction; MASH

    Start
    Weeks 1–4: 0.25 mg; 5–8: 0.5 mg; 9–12: 1 mg; 13–16: 1.7 mg; week 17 on: maintenance. If not tolerated, escalation may be delayed by 4 weeks.
    Maintenance
    Weight: 1.7 or 2.4 mg (2.4 recommended). The label allows 7.2 mg if 2.4 mg was tolerated at least 4 weeks and more loss is needed. CV and MASH: 2.4 mg (or 1.7). Once weekly, subcutaneous.
    Notes
    Missed dose: if the next is more than 2 days away, give as soon as possible; if less, skip. After 2 or more missed doses, restart escalation.

    Label revision: 06/2026 · Sources

    Semaglutide

    Rybelsus (semaglutyd, tabletki)

    Indication: Type 2 diabetes; cardiovascular risk reduction

    Start
    Days 1–30: 3 mg once daily (initiation only). Days 31–60: 7 mg. Day 61 on: 7 mg or 14 mg if needed.
    Maintenance
    Max 14 mg/day. In the morning on an empty stomach with up to 4 oz water, wait at least 30 minutes before food, drink or other oral medicines. Swallow whole. Not interchangeable mg for mg with other semaglutide tablets.
    Notes
    Missed dose: skip, take the next one the following day.

    Foundayo (orforglipron)

    Indication: Weight reduction and long-term maintenance in adults with obesity, or overweight with at least one weight-related condition, with diet and exercise. Not with another GLP-1 receptor agonist.

Secretin, Glucagon, Difelikefalin, Enfuvirtide — Peptide medicines with narrow, specialist uses 🔬

Secretin: diagnosis: pancreatic exocrine insufficiency, gastrinoma, and identifying the papilla during ERCP — approved drug.
Glucagon: severe hypoglycemia in people with diabetes aged 2 and over — approved drug.
Difelikefalin: itch in dialysis patients with chronic kidney disease — approved drug.
Enfuvirtide: HIV-1 resistant to other drugs — approved drug.

Your body makes a hormone that lasts about two minutes 🔬 Somatostatin switches off growth hormone and several gut hormones, then is broken down almost at once. Chemists shortened it from 14 amino acids to 8 and swapped two for mirror-image versions — and octreotide lasts around 100 minutes instead.

Approved in the US in 1988, it is still used for acromegaly and some hormone-producing tumours.

Why does weight loss plateau on GLP-1 medicines?

In the STEP 1 trial, weight loss levelled off at around 60 weeks, at about 15% on semaglutide. The body adapts as weight falls, so talk to your doctor before changing anything. Did you know this? Yes or no.

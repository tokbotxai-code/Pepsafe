If one product heals fifteen different things, that's the tell 🔬 Real medicines get approved for one narrow use at a time, because each new use needs its own trial and its own endpoint. A long benefit list usually means no trial ever tested any of them.

Every claim has a source, or it doesn't — check them at pepsafe.info

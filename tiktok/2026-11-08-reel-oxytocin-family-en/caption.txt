Oxytocin, Carbetocin, Desmopressin, Terlipressin — Hormones from the brain's pituitary, and the medicines built from them 🔬

Oxytocin: labor induction and prevention of postpartum bleeding, given in hospital — approved drug.
Carbetocin: preventing postpartum haemorrhage, especially after caesarean section — approved drug.
Desmopressin: diabetes insipidus, bedwetting, some bleeding disorders — approved drug.
Terlipressin: hepatorenal syndrome — approved drug.

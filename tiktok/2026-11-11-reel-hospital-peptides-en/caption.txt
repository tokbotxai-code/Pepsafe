Nesiritide, Angiotensin II, Ziconotide, Atosiban — Peptide medicines used in hospital care 🔬

Nesiritide: acute heart failure — approved drug.
Angiotensin II: adults with septic or other distributive shock with too-low blood pressure — approved drug.
Ziconotide: severe chronic pain — approved drug.
Atosiban: delaying preterm labour between 24 and 33 weeks of pregnancy — approved drug.

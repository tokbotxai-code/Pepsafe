Romiplostim, Peginesatide, Defibrotide — Peptide-like medicines for blood and platelets 🔬

Romiplostim: chronic immune thrombocytopenia, and acute radiation syndrome — approved drug.
Peginesatide: anaemia of chronic kidney disease in dialysis patients — approved regionally or historically.
Defibrotide: hepatic veno-occlusive disease after a blood stem cell transplant — not a peptide.

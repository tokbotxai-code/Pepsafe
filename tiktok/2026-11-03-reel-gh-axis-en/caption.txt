Somatropin (HGH), Tesamorelin, Sermorelin, Macimorelin — Four medicines tied to the growth hormone axis, and their status 🔬

Somatropin (HGH): growth hormone deficiency in children and adults and other labeled uses — approved drug.
Tesamorelin: abdominal lipodystrophy in people with HIV — approved drug.
Sermorelin: formerly diagnosis and treatment of growth hormone deficiency in children — unapproved.
Macimorelin: diagnosing growth hormone deficiency in adults — approved drug.

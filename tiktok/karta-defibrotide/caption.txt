Defibrotide — Mixture of oligonucleotides (not a peptide) 🔬 A mixture of short DNA fragments obtained from pig intestinal mucosa.

Status: Not a peptide. Evidence: Approved by the EMA in 2013 and the FDA in 2016 for a rare, life-threatening complication.

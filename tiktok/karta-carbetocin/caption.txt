Carbetocin — Oxytocin analogue 🔬 Stimulates oxytocin receptors in the uterus, making it contract and limiting bleeding after birth.

Status: Approved drug. Evidence: Approved in many countries; a heat-stable version is on the WHO list of essential medicines.

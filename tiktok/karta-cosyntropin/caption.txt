Cosyntropin — ACTH analogue 🔬 A shortened version of adrenocorticotropic hormone: the first 24 of its 39 amino acids, which is enough for full activity.

Status: Approved drug. Evidence: A diagnostic standard for decades, described in endocrine guidelines.

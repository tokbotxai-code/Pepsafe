Abarelix — GnRH antagonist 🔬 The first GnRH antagonist approved for prostate cancer.

Status: Approved regionally or historically. Evidence: Phase 3 trials and FDA approval in 2003; the maker withdrew it for commercial reasons and because of allergic reactions.

GDF-11 — TGF-beta family factor 🔬 A protein related to myostatin, famous for a 2013 report that it rejuvenated the heart and muscle of old mice sharing a bloodstream with young ones.

Status: Unapproved. Evidence: The original rejuvenation reports were not confirmed: in 2015 the assays used were shown not to distinguish GDF-11 from myostatin, and later work described the opposite effect.

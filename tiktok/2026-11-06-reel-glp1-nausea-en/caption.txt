Why do GLP-1 medicines cause nausea?

In the STEP 1 trial, about 44% on semaglutide had nausea versus about 17% on placebo. These medicines slow stomach emptying, and nausea is usually mild and tends to fade. Tell your doctor if it persists. Did you know this? Yes or no.

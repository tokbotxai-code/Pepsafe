Carfilzomib — Proteasome inhibitor 🔬 An epoxyketone tetrapeptide that binds the proteasome permanently, unlike the reversible bortezomib.

Status: Approved drug. Evidence: FDA approved in 2012, with phase 3 trials comparing treatment regimens.

Oxytocin, Teriparatide, Salmon calcitonin, Setmelanotide — Four approved peptide medicines you may not have heard of 🔬

Teriparatide: severe osteoporosis — approved drug.
Salmon calcitonin: osteoporosis, Paget's disease, hypercalcemia — approved drug.
Setmelanotide: certain rare genetic forms of obesity — approved drug.

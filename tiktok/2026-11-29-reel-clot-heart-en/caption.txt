Bivalirudin, Eptifibatide, Cangrelor, Nesiritide — Medicines used in heart and clotting care 🔬

Bivalirudin: anticoagulation during coronary procedures — approved drug.
Eptifibatide: acute coronary syndrome and coronary procedures — approved drug.
Cangrelor: percutaneous coronary intervention in patients who have not had an oral antiplatelet drug beforehand — not a peptide.
Nesiritide: acute heart failure — approved drug.

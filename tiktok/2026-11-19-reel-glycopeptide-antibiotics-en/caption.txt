Vancomycin, Daptomycin, Dalbavancin — Peptide-like antibiotics: what they were studied for 🔬

Vancomycin: gram-positive infections, including mrsa — approved drug.
Daptomycin: skin infections, s — approved drug.
Dalbavancin: acute bacterial skin and soft tissue infections in adults caused by susceptible gram-positive bacteria — approved drug.

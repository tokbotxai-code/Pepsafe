Insulins and analogues, Glucagon, Vasopressin, Octreotide, Pramlintide — Five hormones your own body makes, and the medicines built from them 🔬

Insulins and analogues: diabetes — approved drug.
Glucagon: severe hypoglycemia in people with diabetes aged 2 and over — approved drug.
Vasopressin: vasodilatory shock, given in hospital — approved drug.
Octreotide: acromegaly, neuroendocrine tumors — approved drug.
Pramlintide: add-on to mealtime insulin in diabetes — approved drug.

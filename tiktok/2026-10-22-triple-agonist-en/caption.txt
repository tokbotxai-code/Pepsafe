Each generation of these drugs added one more receptor 🔬 The first hit a single gut hormone receptor: appetite down, insulin up. The next added a second and worked better than either alone. The newest adds a third that burns energy instead of storing it — and it is still in trials, because more receptors also means more unknowns.

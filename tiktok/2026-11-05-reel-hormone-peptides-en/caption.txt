Vasopressin, Desmopressin, Somatostatin — Three approved peptide hormone medicines 🔬

Vasopressin: vasodilatory shock, given in hospital — approved drug.
Desmopressin: diabetes insipidus, bedwetting, some bleeding disorders — approved drug.
Somatostatin: bleeding oesophageal varices, pancreatic fistulas, acute gastroenterology settings — approved drug.

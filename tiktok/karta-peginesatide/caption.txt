Peginesatide — Erythropoiesis-stimulating peptide 🔬 A synthetic peptide structurally unrelated to erythropoietin that nonetheless activated the same receptor.

Status: Approved regionally or historically. Evidence: FDA approved in 2012 and withdrawn a year later after reports of severe hypersensitivity reactions, including deaths.

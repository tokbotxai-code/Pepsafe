PantryPal

A calm view of what you have at home.

PantryPal is a quiet pantry list. You add what you have and, if you like, a date. It then shows what to use first. Your data stays in your browser, and you can back it up to a file.

  • Saved on your device, no account needed
  • A “Use first” list built from your dates
  • Shopping list and backup to a file
  • No ads and no trackers

The dates are reminders you entered. PantryPal does not tell you whether food is safe to eat.

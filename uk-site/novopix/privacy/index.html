Privacy policy

This policy covers the contact form on novopix.uk and the free website accessibility check. Polska wersja.

Who is responsible

NovoPix, contact: contact@pepsafe.info.

What data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, e-mail address and the message you write.
  • Free accessibility check: the website address to check, your e-mail address, optionally the name of your organisation, and a record of your consent.

Why and on what basis

  • Replying to your message and preparing a quote: our legitimate interest and taking steps at your request.
  • Preparing and sending a report: providing the service you asked for.
  • Information about other NovoPix services (for example a full audit or monitoring): only with your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by writing to contact@pepsafe.info.

Who receives the data

Technical service providers (e-mail and hosting), only so that the service works. We do not sell data.

How long we keep it

We keep data from the forms for up to 12 months, or until you withdraw consent if that happens sooner.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data and for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or transferred. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or, for Polish residents, to the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Website scanning

We scan only the public website you give us in the form, with a single low-load request.

Cookies and analytics

novopix.uk does not use tracking cookies or advertising. We do not collect analytics about visitors.

An automated first check is not a certificate of compliance or legal advice.

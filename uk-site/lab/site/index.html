How we work

Small teams ship better when the rules are simple.

Tested before it ships

Every change is tried on a phone and on a desktop before it goes out. If it is not ready, it waits.

Private by default

PantryPal keeps your data on your own device. We do not add ads or third-party trackers to our apps.

Plain and honest

Clear wording, real data, no invented numbers. Dates in PantryPal are labels you entered, not safety advice.

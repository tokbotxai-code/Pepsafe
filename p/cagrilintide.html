Cagrilintide

Long-acting amylin analogue

In clinical trials Long-acting amylin analogue Metabolism and weight

What it is

A Novo Nordisk peptide studied mainly together with semaglutide (CagriSema).

How it works

Mimics amylin, a satiety hormone released together with insulin.

Use

Studied in: obesity.

What the research shows

Phase 3; an application for CagriSema has been filed.

What to watch for

The combination with semaglutide is studied in fixed proportions. Mixing it yourself with other products is not the same and there are no data.

    Erythropoietin (EPO)

    Approved drug Protein Anti-doping: S2.1

    What it is

    A hormone that stimulates red blood cell production.

    How it works

    Binds the EPO receptor on blood cell precursors.

    Use

    Anemia in kidney disease and cancer.

    What the research shows

    Approved drug.

    What to watch for

    Raises the risk of clots and high blood pressure.

    Anti-doping

    Listed by WADA in class S2.1, prohibited at all times. WADA

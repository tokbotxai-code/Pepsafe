Erythropoietin (EPO)

Protein

Approved drug Protein Heart and clotting WADA list: S2.1

What it is

A hormone that stimulates red blood cell production.

How it works

Binds the EPO receptor on blood cell precursors.

Use

Anemia in kidney disease and cancer.

What the research shows

Approved drug.

What to watch for

Raises the risk of clots and high blood pressure.

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S2.1, prohibited at all times. WADA list

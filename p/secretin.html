Secretin

ChiRhoStim

Approved drug Gut hormone (peptide) Gut

What it is

Synthetic human secretin given IV for diagnosis.

How it works

Stimulates the pancreas to secrete juice including bicarbonate, and gastrin release in a diagnostic test.

Use

Diagnosis: pancreatic exocrine insufficiency, gastrinoma, and identifying the papilla during ERCP.

What the research shows

Used as a diagnostic agent.

What to watch for

Label warnings: falsely low results after vagotomy, in inflammatory bowel disease and with anticholinergic drugs; falsely high with PPIs, H2 blockers or liver disease.

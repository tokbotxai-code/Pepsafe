CJC-1295

CJC-1295 DAC, Mod GRF 1-29

Unapproved GHRH analogue Hormones WADA list: S2.2

What it is

A modified peptide stimulating the growth hormone axis.

How it works

Acts on the GHRH receptor; the DAC version binds albumin and lasts longer.

Use

Studied experimentally.

What the research shows

Not approved, very scarce clinical data.

What to watch for

Affects the growth hormone axis; no long-term safety data. @NC

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S2.2, prohibited at all times. WADA list

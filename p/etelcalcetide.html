Etelcalcetide

Parsabiv

Approved drug Calcimimetic peptide Hormones

What it is

FDA-approved in 2017. Given IV at the end of hemodialysis.

How it works

Stimulates the calcium-sensing receptor in the parathyroids and lowers parathyroid hormone.

Use

Secondary hyperparathyroidism in adults with chronic kidney disease on hemodialysis.

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in dialysis patients.

What to watch for

Label warnings: low calcium, worsening heart failure, upper GI bleeding, adynamic bone disease. Only under a specialist.

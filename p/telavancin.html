Telavancin

Vibativ

Approved drug Lipoglycopeptide (antibiotic) Infections

What it is

FDA-approved in 2009. An antibiotic given by IV infusion.

How it works

A lipoglycopeptide that blocks cell wall synthesis in Gram-positive bacteria.

Use

Complicated skin infections in adults and hospital-acquired pneumonia, including ventilator-associated, caused by susceptible Staphylococcus aureus. For pneumonia only when other treatment is not suitable.

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in skin and lung infections.

What to watch for

Label warnings: higher mortality in pneumonia with kidney impairment, kidney toxicity, fetal risk, QTc prolongation, hypersensitivity, infusion reactions, C. difficile diarrhea.

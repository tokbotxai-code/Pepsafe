Glatiramer

Copaxone

Approved drug Synthetic amino acid polymer Immunity

What it is

A random mixture of polypeptides from glutamate, lysine, alanine and tyrosine (1996).

How it works

Modulates T-cell responses against myelin.

Use

Relapsing multiple sclerosis.

What the research shows

Approved drug.

What to watch for

Use under a neurologist.

Dihexa

PNB-0408

Unapproved Nootropic peptide Neurology

What it is

A modified, short angiotensin IV analog developed at Washington State University.

How it works

Binds hepatocyte growth factor (HGF) and boosts its c-Met receptor, which promoted new connections between neurons in animal studies.

Use

No approved uses. Studied for dementia.

What the research shows

Animal (rat) studies only. The related molecule fosgonimeton did not improve outcomes in a trial in Alzheimer's patients.

What to watch for

The HGF/c-Met pathway is involved in tumour growth, and long-term safety has never been studied. @ND

Desmopressin

DDAVP

Approved drug Vasopressin analogue Heart and clotting WADA list: S5

What it is

A modified vasopressin analogue with a stronger kidney effect (1978).

How it works

Activates the V2 receptor and reduces urine output.

Use

Diabetes insipidus, bedwetting, some bleeding disorders.

What the research shows

Approved drug.

What to watch for

Risk of low sodium (hyponatremia). Caution with drugs causing SIADH and diuretics.

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S5, prohibited at all times. WADA list

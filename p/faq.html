    Questions

    Questions, answered plainly.

    32 questions about peptides, hormones and anabolic drugs, answered from the research — and about what we will not answer.

    Are peptides proteins?

    Peptides and proteins are made of the same amino acids. Peptides are shorter, proteins longer and folded into a stable structure. The border is conventional, often around 50 amino acids.

    Do peptides in creams reach the deeper skin?

    It depends on the molecule and carrier. Large molecules cross the epidermis poorly, so makers modify peptides, for example by attaching a fatty acid. Much of the data comes from lab studies.

    Why are peptide drugs usually injected?

    Peptides are digested by gut acid and enzymes, trapped by mucus and cross the gut lining poorly. An exception is semaglutide tablets, approved in 2019, which work with an absorption enhancer.

    Are peptides bought online safe?

    “Research only” products are not controlled like drugs and human data on them are scarce. Details are in the blog article.

    Does collagen powder work?

    A 2023 meta-analysis points to a modest gain in skin elasticity and hydration in healthy adults, but trials were small and partly subjective. It does not replace treatment.

    Where can I buy peptides?

    Approved peptide drugs are available only by prescription, from a pharmacy, after a doctor's decision. Substances sold as “research only” are not medicines: they lack drug-grade quality control and are not approved for human use. This site lists no shops, links or prices for peptides.

    How are lyophilized peptides stored?

    General rule for lyophilized powders: cool, dry, tightly sealed, away from light and moisture. After reconstitution peptides are usually much less stable. For a medicine, the exact conditions are in its leaflet, not in general rules.

    What is the difference between purity and peptide content?

    Purity (e.g. by HPLC) tells what share of the peptide material is the target peptide rather than related peptides. Peptide content tells what share of the vial mass is peptide at all, the rest being salts, water and excipients. High purity therefore does not mean the labelled mass is all active substance.

    How do I read a certificate of analysis (CoA)?

    Check: name and batch number matching the package, date, method (HPLC for purity, mass spectrometry for identity), result and who tested. A certificate issued by the seller without an independent lab proves little, and a CoA neither makes a substance a medicine nor replaces quality control.

    Can I ask about a dose or how to use something?

    We do not answer those. For most substances in the catalog no safe human dose has been established, and the numbers circulating on forums and from sellers are guesswork. We give doses only for approved drugs, copied from current labels, in the Dosing tab.

    Which seller is trustworthy?

    We do not recommend sellers and we do not run rankings. “Research” products are not medicines and nobody checks their contents or sterility. The Alerts tab collects FDA documents and studies showing what such products contain.

    What does “for research purposes only” mean?

    To the FDA, nothing. In warning letters the agency stated plainly that such a label does not change a product’s status when everything around it shows it is meant for people: effect claims, guides, calculators or bacteriostatic water sold alongside.

    I have a worrying symptom after a peptide. What should I do?

    We cannot assess symptoms over a message and we do not replace a doctor. For severe or sudden symptoms call emergency services. Tell the doctor exactly what was taken and when, even if it came from the internet. You can also describe the symptom in our form; reports are not published.

    Does a certificate of analysis (COA) mean a product is safe?

    No. A COA describes one sample, not a whole batch, and says nothing about sterility or whether the tested sample came from what you receive. Such documents also get copied between sellers. Independent analyses of products bought online found purity in the low tens of percent against an advertised 99.

    Is peptide X banned in sport?

    Check the anti-doping checker in the Catalog tab. It shows the WADA class and whether the ban applies at all times. Watch for the S0 clause: it covers substances with no approval for human treatment, even when they are not named.

    Why don’t you say something works when people say it helped them?

    Because individual reports cannot separate a substance’s effect from time, rehabilitation, a change in training or placebo. That is why each entry shows how many human trials are actually registered, with sources so anyone can check.

    Can I advertise my product here?

    No. We do not work with peptide sellers and we do not publish ads or shop links. The catalog is free and funded by our optional PDF guides, with no commission from anyone.

    What is the difference between anabolic steroids and corticosteroids?

    Anabolic steroids copy testosterone and build muscle and bone. Corticosteroids, such as prednisone, copy cortisol and calm inflammation and the immune system. They are different drugs with different risks, and they are often mixed up.

    Are peptides steroids?

    No. Peptides are short chains of amino acids, while steroids are fat-derived hormones with a four-ring core. Insulin and semaglutide are peptide drugs and neither is a steroid.

    Are anabolic steroids legal in the UK and the US?

    They are controlled drugs: Class C in the UK and Schedule III in the US. A doctor can prescribe some of them, such as testosterone, for a diagnosed condition, but supplying them without authority is an offence in both countries.

    Is it a crime to have steroids for personal use in the UK?

    In the UK, having anabolic steroids for your own use is not an offence in itself, but producing, supplying or offering to supply them is, and the rules on importing are strict. In the US, having them without a prescription is a federal offence. Laws change, so check current official guidance.

    What does “anabolic-androgenic” mean?

    Anabolic means building tissue, such as muscle and bone. Androgenic means producing male traits, such as a deeper voice and body hair. Every anabolic steroid has both effects, in different proportions.

    Is medical testosterone the same as steroid misuse?

    No. Testosterone replacement is a prescribed treatment for a proven deficiency, aimed at bringing levels back to the normal range and monitored with blood tests. Misuse means taking androgens without a medical reason, often in amounts far above what a doctor would prescribe.

    What are SARMs, and are they steroids?

    SARMs, or selective androgen receptor modulators, are lab compounds meant to act on muscle and bone more selectively than steroids. They are not steroids, none is approved as a medicine, and regulators have warned about products sold under these names.

    Can steroids cause permanent damage?

    Some effects can last. A deeper voice and some body-hair changes in women often do not reverse, heart changes can persist, and recovery of natural testosterone and fertility can be slow or incomplete. Teenagers who are still growing can end up shorter because the growth plates close early.

    Do steroids affect fertility?

    Yes. Anabolic steroids tell the brain to stop the signals that make the testes work, so sperm production can fall sharply. In many men it recovers within months after stopping, but in some it takes much longer or does not fully return.

    Why are steroids prohibited in sport?

    They give an unfair advantage and carry health risks, so WADA bans anabolic agents at all times, in and out of competition. A first intentional violation can bring a ban of up to four years. An athlete who needs a banned medicine for a real illness can apply for a therapeutic use exemption beforehand.

    Why do some prescription drugs for breast cancer and fertility appear in steroid discussions?

    Medicines such as clomiphene, tamoxifen and anastrozole change oestrogen or the signals that drive testosterone, so people connect them with steroids. They are real prescription drugs with approved uses and documented side effects, and we give no guidance on using them alongside steroids.

    Is it true that steroids cause “roid rage”?

    Some people report irritability, aggression and mood swings during use, and low mood when levels fall afterwards. Research is mixed on how common severe effects are, and not everyone is affected in the same way.

    Do steroids affect women differently?

    Women are more sensitive to the masculinising effects of androgens. A deeper voice, thicker body and facial hair, disturbed periods and an enlarged clitoris can occur, and some of these changes do not reverse when the drug is stopped.

    What is DNP, and why is it so dangerous?

    DNP, or 2,4-dinitrophenol, is an industrial chemical that makes cells waste energy as heat. It can cause fatal overheating, there is no antidote, and the gap between a harmful and a deadly amount is small. It is not a steroid or a medicine, and selling it for human use is illegal.

    Why do you not say where to get steroids or how to use them?

    PepSafe is an education site. We give facts, risks and legal status, but no doses, no instructions for use and no places to buy. Products from the illegal market can be counterfeit, mislabelled or contaminated, and the safe route to any hormone treatment is a doctor.

Melatonin

Hormone, not a peptide

Not a peptide Hormone, not a peptide Neurology WADA list:

What it is

A pineal gland hormone.

How it works

Binds MT1 and MT2 receptors.

Use

Sleep problems; a drug or supplement depending on the country.

What the research shows

Moderate evidence in selected sleep-rhythm disorders.

What to watch for

May increase drowsiness. Check interactions with a pharmacist.

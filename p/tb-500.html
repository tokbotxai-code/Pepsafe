TB-500

Thymosin β4 fragment, LKKTETQ

Unapproved “Research” peptide Recovery and “research” WADA list: S2.3

What it is

A fragment of natural thymosin beta-4.

How it works

Hypothesis: binds actin and affects cell migration in healing.

Use

Studied preclinically (healing, regeneration).

What the research shows

Mostly animal data. An FDA advisory committee backed it in July 2026, without binding effect.

Somatropin (HGH)

Genotropin, Norditropin, Omnitrope

Approved drug Protein (191 amino acids) Hormones WADA list: S2.2

What it is

Recombinant growth hormone. Structurally a protein, not a short peptide.

How it works

Binds the growth hormone receptor and makes the liver produce IGF-1.

Use

Growth hormone deficiency in children and adults and other labeled uses.

What the research shows

Approved prescription drug.

What to watch for

Only under an endocrinologist. Excess can cause insulin resistance and fluid retention, among others.

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S2.2, prohibited at all times. WADA list

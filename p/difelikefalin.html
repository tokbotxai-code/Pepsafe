Korsuva

Approved drug Kappa opioid receptor agonist Neurology

What it is

A peptide acting outside the central nervous system, approved in 2021.

How it works

Binds peripheral kappa receptors and reduces itch.

Use

Itch in dialysis patients with chronic kidney disease.

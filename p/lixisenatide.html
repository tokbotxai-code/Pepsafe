Lixisenatide

Adlyxin

Approved drug GLP-1 agonist Metabolism and weight

What it is

A short-acting GLP-1 agonist approved in 2016.

How it works

Binds the GLP-1 receptor, with a stronger effect on after-meal glucose.

Use

Type 2 diabetes.

What the research shows

Phase 3 trials.

What to watch for

Do not combine with other GLP-1 agonists or tirzepatide. Hypoglycemia with insulin or a sulfonylurea.

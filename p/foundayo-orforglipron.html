Foundayo (orforglipron)

Orforglipron, Lilly

Not a peptide Oral GLP-1 agonist (small molecule, not a peptide) Metabolism and weight

What it is

A once-daily tablet taken at any time, with or without food. FDA-approved on 1 April 2026 for chronic weight management.

How it works

Activates the GLP-1 receptor like peptide drugs, but as a small molecule it needs no injections or special absorption conditions.

Use

Chronic weight management in adults with obesity, or overweight with a related condition (US).

What the research shows

ATTAIN-1: mean weight loss 11.1% vs 2.1% on placebo at 72 weeks.

What to watch for

Do not combine with other GLP-1 agonists or tirzepatide. Boxed warning on thyroid C-cell tumors as with other GLP-1 drugs. Prescription only.

    Buserelin

    Suprefact, Profact

    Approved drug GnRH agonist

    Group

    GnRH axis (agonists, antagonists and stimulators)

    Do not combine on your own

    Group

    What it is

    A synthetic analogue of gonadotropin-releasing hormone, far more potent than the natural one. Given by injection or as a nasal spray.

    How it works

    At first it stimulates the pituitary to release sex hormones, then continuous dosing shuts it down: the receptors stop responding and testosterone or estrogen falls.

    Use

    Prostate cancer, endometriosis, uterine fibroids, preparation for IVF.

    What the research shows

    An approved medicine since the 1980s, widely used in urology and gynaecology.

    What to watch for

    Symptoms may briefly worsen at the start, because hormones rise before they fall. Hot flushes, lowered libido, bone loss with long use. Prescription only, supervised by a specialist.

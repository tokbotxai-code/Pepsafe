    Pegvisomant

    Somavert

    Approved drug Growth hormone receptor antagonist

    Group

    Growth hormone and IGF-1 axis

    Do not combine: same hormonal axis

    Group

    What it is

    A modified growth hormone protein with polyethylene glycol chains attached. It does the opposite of the hormone: it blocks instead of stimulating.

    How it works

    Binds the growth hormone receptor without activating it, so the liver stops producing IGF-1.

    Use

    Acromegaly, when surgery and other drugs have not been enough.

    What the research shows

    Approved by the FDA and EMA, with trials showing IGF-1 normalised in most patients.

    What to watch for

    Needs regular liver function and IGF-1 checks. It does not shrink the pituitary tumour, so tumour size is monitored separately. It is not an anti-growth-hormone product for body composition.

Exenatide

Byetta, Bydureon

Approved drug GLP-1 agonist Metabolism and weight

What it is

The first approved GLP-1 agonist (2005). Derived from exendin-4, a peptide from Gila monster saliva.

How it works

Binds the GLP-1 receptor and increases insulin release after meals.

Use

Type 2 diabetes.

What the research shows

Phase 3 trials, long experience.

What to watch for

Do not combine with other GLP-1 agonists or tirzepatide. Hypoglycemia with insulin or a sulfonylurea.

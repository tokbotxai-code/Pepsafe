Dasiglucagon

Zegalogue

Approved drug Glucagon analogue Hormones

What it is

FDA-approved in 2021. Subcutaneous injection for severe hypoglycemia.

How it works

Acts like glucagon and raises blood glucose.

Use

Severe hypoglycemia in people with diabetes (current label: weighing at least 20 kg).

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in diabetes.

What to watch for

Label warnings: raised blood pressure with pheochromocytoma, hypoglycemia with insulinoma, allergic reactions, lack of effect with low liver glycogen.

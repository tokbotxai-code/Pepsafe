BPC-157 + TB-500

BPC+TB, GLOW, KLOW

Unapproved Blends of “research” peptides Recovery and “research” WADA list: S0 i S2.3

What it is

Products combining several substances, never studied together. GLOW is usually GHK-Cu, BPC-157 and TB-500, and KLOW (e.g. KLOW 80) adds KPV. Composition and ratios vary between sellers.

How it works

No studies of the mechanism of blends.

Use

None.

What the research shows

No studies of such combinations exist.

What to watch for

Each ingredient has scarce data, and nobody has studied them together.

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S0 i S2.3, prohibited at all times. WADA list

Tirzepatide

Mounjaro, Zepbound

Approved drug GIP and GLP-1 agonist Metabolism and weight WADA list:

What it is

A peptide acting on two gut hormone receptors, given once weekly.

How it works

Activates GIP and GLP-1 receptors, increasing insulin release, reducing appetite and slowing stomach emptying.

Use

Type 2 diabetes (Mounjaro), obesity treatment (Zepbound).

What the research shows

Large phase 3 trials and approvals.

What to watch for

The maker (Lilly) states there are no clinical data on combining it with another GLP-1 agonist; both would act on the same receptors, which may increase side effects without added benefit. Per the label it may reduce the effect of oral contraceptives after starting and after dose increases. Hypoglycemia risk with insulin or a sulfonylurea.

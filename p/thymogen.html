    Thymogen

    Thymogen, Glu-Trp

    Approved regionally or historically Thymic dipeptide

    What it is

    A two-amino-acid peptide matching the smallest active fragment of thymosin alpha-1. Registered as a medicine in Russia and a few neighbouring countries.

    How it works

    Described as a regulator of T cell maturation; mechanistic data are thin.

    Use

    In the countries where it is registered: supportive use in infections and immune deficiency.

    What the research shows

    A regional registration with no FDA or EMA equivalent. Most studies predate 1991, are hard to obtain and rarely have a control group.

    What to watch for

    Being a medicine in one country does not mean the evidence meets today's standards. Not legally available in the European Union. Human safety is not established beyond those registrations.

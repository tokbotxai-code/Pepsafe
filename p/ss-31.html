SS-31

Elamipretyd, Forzinity

Approved drug Mitochondrial peptide Other

What it is

A tetrapeptide targeting the mitochondrial membrane.

How it works

Binds cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane and stabilises it.

Use

Barth syndrome (rare genetic disease), FDA accelerated approval in 2025.

What the research shows

Approved for this one use. Other uses are not confirmed.

What to watch for

Use only for the approved indication under a doctor.

Adipotide

FTPP

Unapproved Peptidomimetic targeting fat tissue blood vessels Metabolism and weight

What it is

A peptide aimed at blood vessels in fat tissue.

How it works

Binds a protein on fat tissue vessel lining and kills those vessel cells.

Use

Studied experimentally.

What the research shows

Animal studies, including monkeys. Kidney toxicity was reported.

What to watch for

@ND

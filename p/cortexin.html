    Cortexin

    Cortexin

    Approved regionally or historically Peptide extract from brain cortex

    Group

    “Brain and mood” peptides (unapproved or regional)

    Do not combine: same claimed goal, no data

    Group

    What it is

    A mixture of peptides obtained from cattle and pig brain cortex, registered as a medicine in Russia. It is not a single substance but an extract of variable composition.

    How it works

    The maker describes neuroprotection and improved neuronal metabolism. Because the composition is not fully defined, the mechanism cannot be pinned down.

    Use

    In the countries where it is registered: after stroke, in encephalopathy, in children with developmental delay.

    What the research shows

    No studies meeting international standards. It is registered with neither the FDA nor the EMA.

    In depth

    Cortexin is a mixture of peptides obtained from the brain cortex of cattle and pigs, registered as a medicine in Russia for stroke, encephalopathy and children with developmental delay, among others.

    The extract's composition is not fully defined, and the studies are mainly Russian. Animal brain preparations also carry a separate infection risk, including prion disease, which is why similar extracts were withdrawn in the EU. Outside Russia it is not a medicine.

    Advantages

    • Registered in some countries for use after stroke and in encephalopathy
    • Used in children with developmental delay where licensed

    Disadvantages

    • No trials that meet international standards
    • Not approved by the FDA or EMA

    What to watch for

    Animal brain-derived preparations carry a separate infection risk, including prion disease, which is why such extracts were withdrawn in the European Union. Human safety is not established.

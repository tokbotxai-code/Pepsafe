Insulins and analogues

Humalog, NovoLog, Apidra, Lantus, Levemir, Tresiba

Approved drug Peptide hormone (2 chains) Metabolism and weight WADA list: S4.4

What it is

Human insulin has 51 amino acids in two chains (21 and 30) joined by disulfide bridges. Analogues change single amino acids to act faster or longer.

How it works

Binds the insulin receptor and triggers glucose uptake into cells.

Use

Diabetes.

What the research shows

Approved since 1982 (human insulin), analogues since the 1990s.

What to watch for

Hypoglycemia, especially with GLP-1 agonists or a sulfonylurea. Doses are set only by a doctor.

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S4.4, prohibited at all times. WADA list

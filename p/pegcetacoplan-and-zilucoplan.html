Pegcetacoplan and zilucoplan

Empaveli, Zilbrysq

Approved drug Complement inhibitors Immunity

What it is

Peptides blocking the complement system (2021 and 2023). Zilucoplan is a 15-amino-acid macrocyclic peptide injected under the skin.

How it works

Inhibit complement components (C3 or C5). Zilucoplan binds C5 and blocks its cleavage.

Use

Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria (pegcetacoplan). Generalised myasthenia gravis in adults with anti-AChR antibodies (zilucoplan, per FDA label).

What the research shows

Approved drugs.

What to watch for

Zilucoplan: boxed warning for serious meningococcal infections (REMS program), also other infections and pancreatitis. Vaccinations required. Only under a specialist.

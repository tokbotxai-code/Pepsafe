Romidepsin

Istodax

Approved drug Cyclic depsipeptide (anticancer) Oncology

What it is

FDA-approved in 2009. A cyclic depsipeptide given by IV.

How it works

An anticancer drug that inhibits histone deacetylases.

Use

Cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (CTCL) in adults after at least one prior systemic therapy.

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in CTCL.

What to watch for

Label warnings: bone marrow suppression, infections, ECG changes, tumor lysis syndrome, fetal toxicity. Only under an oncologist.

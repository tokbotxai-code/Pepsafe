Dalbavancin

Dalvance

Approved drug Lipoglycopeptide (antibiotic) Infections

What it is

FDA-approved in 2014. An antibiotic given IV.

How it works

A lipoglycopeptide that blocks cell wall synthesis in Gram-positive bacteria.

Use

Acute bacterial skin and soft tissue infections in adults caused by susceptible Gram-positive bacteria.

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in skin infections.

What to watch for

Label warnings: hypersensitivity, infusion reactions, liver effects, C. difficile diarrhea, bacterial resistance.

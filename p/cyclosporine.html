Cyclosporine

Sandimmune

Approved drug Cyclic peptide (immunosuppressant) Immunity

What it is

A cyclic 11-amino-acid peptide, an oral and IV immunosuppressant.

How it works

An immunosuppressant that limits the immune response.

Use

With corticosteroids: prevention of kidney, liver and heart transplant rejection, and chronic rejection in patients previously treated with other immunosuppressants (per Sandimmune label).

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in transplant patients.

What to watch for

Label warnings: kidney toxicity, thrombotic microangiopathy, high potassium, liver toxicity, cancers, serious infections, polyoma virus infections, neurotoxicity, anaphylaxis. Boxed: use recommendations, combination with corticosteroids, risk of wrong product substitution and blood level monitoring. Only under a specialist.

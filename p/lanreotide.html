Lanreotide

Somatuline

Approved drug Somatostatin analogue Oncology

What it is

A long-acting somatostatin analogue (2007).

How it works

Binds somatostatin receptors.

Use

Acromegaly, neuroendocrine tumors.

What the research shows

Approved drug.

What to watch for

Affects blood glucose. Use under a doctor.

    Albiglutide

    Tanzeum, Eperzan

    Approved regionally or historically GLP-1 agonist

    Group

    GLP-1 agonists and related (GIP, glucagon)

    Do not combine: duplicated mechanism

    Group

    What it is

    A GLP-1 agonist fused to human albumin, given once weekly. Withdrawn in 2018 for commercial reasons, not safety ones.

    How it works

    Bound the GLP-1 receptor, increasing glucose-dependent insulin release and slowing stomach emptying.

    Use

    Type 2 diabetes (a historical indication).

    What the research shows

    Approved by the FDA and EMA in 2014; the HARMONY Outcomes trial showed cardiovascular benefit after the maker had already decided to withdraw it.

    BPC-157

    Body protection compound

    Unapproved “Research” peptide Anti-doping: S0

    Group

    “Recovery” peptides (unapproved)

    Do not combine: same claimed goal, no data

    Group

    What it is

    A 15-amino-acid peptide described as derived from a gastric juice protein. The most talked-about “research” peptide.

    How it works

    Hypotheses from animal studies: effects on blood vessels and tissue healing. The mechanism in humans is not confirmed.

    Use

    Studied mainly in animals (tissue healing, gut).

    What the research shows

    Humans: per a 2025 review only 3 studies, under 30 people in total, no control groups. An FDA advisory committee backed it in July 2026 (8 to 6), but that is not approval, and FDA staff were against.

    In depth

    BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid peptide described as a fragment of a protein from gastric juice. Almost all studies come from one team in Zagreb (Predrag Sikirić), which since the 1990s has published rat results: faster healing of tendons, muscles, gut and blood vessels.

    Human data are very thin: a 2025 review found only three small studies, under 30 people in total, without control groups. In 2023 the FDA placed BPC-157 in the category of compounding substances with safety concerns; in July 2026 an FDA advisory committee voted 8 to 6 to allow it for compounding, which is not drug approval. WADA has prohibited it since 2022 (class S0). Products from the internet have no sterility or content control.

    Advantages

    • Many animal studies on tissue and gut healing
    • FDA advisory committee backed it in 2026, though not binding

    Disadvantages

    • Only three small human studies, under 30 people in total, without controls
    • Promotes new blood vessels, which could matter with cancer
    • Not approved and doses are not established

    What we do not know

    There are no proper controlled trials of safety in people. Almost all evidence comes from rats, and because it promotes the growth of blood vessels, researchers worry in theory about tumours, which has not been tested.

    What to watch for

    No established doses. Stimulates blood vessel growth, which could in theory matter with cancer. There are no studied combinations with medicines or other peptides. Combining them is a doctor's decision.

    Anti-doping

    Listed by WADA in class S0, prohibited at all times. WADA

Semaglutide

Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus

Approved drug GLP-1 agonist Metabolism and weight WADA list:

What it is

A chemically modified peptide similar to human GLP-1, with additions that prolong its action. Given once weekly by injection or daily as a tablet with an absorption enhancer.

How it works

Binds the GLP-1 receptor: increases glucose-dependent insulin release, slows stomach emptying and reduces appetite through the brain.

Use

Type 2 diabetes, obesity treatment, cardiovascular risk reduction (depending on product).

What the research shows

Large phase 3 trials and regulatory approvals.

What to watch for

Do not combine with another GLP-1 agonist or tirzepatide: duplicated mechanism with no proven benefit. With insulin or a sulfonylurea the risk of hypoglycemia rises; medication changes are a doctor's decision. Counterfeit products have been reported.

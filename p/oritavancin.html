Oritavancin

Orbactiv, Kimyrsa

Approved drug Lipoglycopeptide (antibiotic) Infections

What it is

FDA-approved in 2014. An antibiotic given as a single IV infusion.

How it works

A lipoglycopeptide that blocks cell wall synthesis in Gram-positive bacteria.

Use

Acute bacterial skin and soft tissue infections in adults.

What the research shows

Approved based on clinical trials in skin infections.

What to watch for

Label warnings: interference with coagulation tests, hypersensitivity, infusion reactions, C. difficile diarrhea, bleeding risk with warfarin, osteomyelitis.

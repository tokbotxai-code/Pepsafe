Ipamorelin

Growth hormone secretagogue

Unapproved Growth hormone secretagogue Hormones WADA list: S2.2

What it is

A synthetic pentapeptide.

How it works

Acts on the ghrelin receptor and stimulates growth hormone release.

Use

Studied for gut motility problems after surgery; development stopped.

What the research shows

Not approved.

What to watch for

Affects the growth hormone axis. @NC

Anti-doping status

Listed by WADA in class S2.2, prohibited at all times. WADA list

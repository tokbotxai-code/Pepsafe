Ziconotide

Prialt

Approved drug Cone snail venom peptide Neurology

What it is

A synthetic version of a Conus magus peptide. Given into the spinal canal.

How it works

Blocks N-type calcium channels in pain-transmitting neurons.

Use

Severe chronic pain (2004).

What the research shows

Approved drug.

What to watch for

Warnings of psychiatric and cognitive effects. Only in a medical center.

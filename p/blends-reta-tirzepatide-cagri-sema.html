Blends: Reta + Tirzepatide, Cagri + Sema

Two substances in one vial

Unapproved Two substances in one vial Metabolism and weight

What it is

Vials combining two substances with related action.

How it works

Both ingredients act on GLP-1 (and GIP) receptors, so effects overlap.

Use

No justified use.

What the research shows

No studies. CagriSema is an investigational product with a fixed formula; a vial blend is not its equivalent.

What to watch for

Reta + tirzepatide doubles action on GIP and GLP-1. There are no safety data on such a mix.
